Fighting food insecurity in our community

GreenLeaf Community Food Bank collects, sorts, and distributes food donations to low-income families in our local area. We run a weekly food parcel service, a weekend soup kitchen, and a drop-off service for businesses.

What we do

Weekly food parcel distribution

Pre-packed parcels of staples and seasonal produce, handed out every Tuesday and Thursday.

Weekend soup kitchen

A hot, sit-down meal open to anyone who needs one, every Saturday and Sunday.

Donation drop-off & collection

Drop food off at the depot or a partner bin, or arrange a recurring pickup.
