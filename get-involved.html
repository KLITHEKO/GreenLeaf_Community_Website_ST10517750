Get involved

Whether you have five minutes, five hours, or a standing order to make, there's a place for you at GreenLeaf.

Donate funds or food

Cash donations let us buy exactly what's short on the shelf this week. Prefer to give food directly? Drop it at the depot or a partner bin anytime.

Volunteer with us

No experience needed - just reliable hands. Sort parcels, serve at the soup kitchen, or help run collection routes.

Partner your business

Restaurants, grocers, and offices can set up a recurring pickup for surplus, near-date, or unsellable-but-safe food.

Request a food parcel

Parcels are available to anyone facing food insecurity in the area. No paperwork is required for a first visit - just come to a distribution site during the hours listed on our Services page.
